Privacy policy

Thali Coach · Android app com.thalicoach.app and website · Effective 8 November 2026

Thali Coach is an offline, educational diet and activity companion for Indian home cooking. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to your information. The short version: your data stays on your device, and the app has no account, no server and no analytics.

1. What the app stores

Everything you enter is saved in your device's local app storage (localStorage inside the app or browser):

  • your setup answers — age, sex, height, weight, activity, diet, budget and safety answers;
  • your meal ticks, water, activity, weight and waist check-ins;
  • your food-coach text and the estimated food log it produces;
  • your grocery and prep ticks, reminder times and app preferences.

These entries are not uploaded anywhere. The developer cannot read them, and no other app can read them through Thali Coach.

2. What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, email address or phone number is required.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone.
  • No cookies are used for tracking.
  • The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, files or location.

3. Voice input (website only)

On the website, the food coach can listen to what you say using your browser's built-in speech recognition. That feature is provided by the browser (for example Google Chrome), and the browser company may process the audio on its own servers under its privacy policy. Thali Coach does not record, store or receive the audio, and the recognised text is handled exactly like text you type. Voice input is unavailable inside the Android app shell, so the microphone button is hidden there.

4. Notifications

Reminders are generated on your device while the app is open or running. There is no push server and no notification data leaves the phone. You can switch every reminder off in the app or block notifications in Android settings.

5. Links to other sites

The app may link to a web page (for example this policy or a download page). Those sites have their own privacy practices; this policy covers Thali Coach only.

6. Children and teenagers

Thali Coach is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13. If a user is under 18, the app automatically switches to healthy-habits mode and hides calorie deficits and weight targets. Please do not use the app to replace advice from a doctor or registered dietitian.

7. Health disclaimer

Thali Coach is a general educational tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease and is not medical advice. All calorie, protein, weight and portion numbers are rough estimates from standard food tables, not measurements. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a medical condition such as diabetes, thyroid, PCOS, kidney or heart disease, take medication, have a history of disordered eating, or are under 18, please get individual guidance before changing how you eat.

8. How to delete your data

You are in control of the data on your device:

  • Website: clear site data for this site in your browser settings (or use a private window).
  • Android app: open Android Settings → Apps → Thali Coach → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app later adds an online feature, this page will be updated before that feature is released, and the effective date above will change.

10. Contact

Questions or concerns about privacy: open an issue at github.com/Santollamanikanta/thali-coach/issues. Please do not include personal health details in a public issue.
